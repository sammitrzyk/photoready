Frequently asked questions

Why spend money on a house I'm leaving?+

Because condition shows up in the offers. We focus on what buyers notice in photos and showings, like clutter, dated walls, dirt and curb appeal, and skip upgrades that won't pay back.

Do I need to know what the house needs?+

No. We walk through with you, then hand you one plan and one price. You decide what's in and what's out.

What happens to my things?+

You choose, room by room: keep, store, donate or toss. We handle the packing, moving and hauling.

How does payment work?+

Two ways: pay nothing until closing through our financing partner Notable (subject to approval), or pay half when we start and half when the work is done.

Will you work with my agent?+

Yes. We coordinate the schedule with your agent so everyone knows exactly when the house is ready to list.

Do you only take big projects?+

No. Some homes need a full cleanout and paint; some just need one room cleared and a final clean. The plan fits the house.
